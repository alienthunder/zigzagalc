ZigZag is a community that celebrates child-led education through building trust, creativity, and authentic connection

We provide a mini-campus, rich in classes and culture, where kids are welcome to be fully themselves, where curiosity leads choices, and where learning unfolds joyfully in extraordinary ways.

Trust kids. Then watch what they build.

ZigZag is Asheville’s Agile Learning Community: a self-directed school for kids 4 to 18, on three acres of fields, trees, creek and ponds near Bent Creek. Nobody hands kids a script for what’s worth learning. They write their own.

Every morning starts in Pods, where kids check in, set intentions and build our culture together. Then the day is theirs: robotics or aerial silks, a play in rehearsal, Kid Café, a boat half-built, a game in the Zone, or a long talk in the hammock. Every hour is a choice.

Self-direction isn’t something we hand out. It’s a skill, and kids practice it here every day: choosing, committing, following through, and working out conflict together. That’s how they discover they can get really good at something they picked themselves.

Underneath it all is trust: in kids, in each other, and in the families who build this place with us.
